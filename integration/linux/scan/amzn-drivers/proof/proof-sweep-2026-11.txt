properties:

              `fixed` are SUCCESSFUL; `nb_positive` fails by design (the
              PTX_NB_POSITIVE claim is false, as documented).

              fail -- the three F14 reproductions.  `no_b1`,
              `no_meta_disabled_no_offl`, `no_pushed_only` and `fixed` are
              SUCCESSFUL, which is the table in the F14 writeup.
  reset_flag  the stale-flag configuration fails F21_WD_RUNS -- that is F21.
              The `control_no_reset` and `with_fix` configurations are
              SUCCESSFUL, which is what shows the harness measures the stale
              flag and not something else.
  rss_reta    the default configuration fails the indirect_table bound -- that
              is F19.  -DWITH_UPDATE_CHECK, the proposed fix, is SUCCESSFUL.
  conc_admin  the arbitrary-device configuration fails CC2_NO_STALE_CQE;
              -DWELL_BEHAVED_DEVICE is SUCCESSFUL.  That difference is the
              result, recorded as an observation.
