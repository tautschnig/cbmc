24 of 24 runner claims stand.

Getting to that number took four fixes to the ADJUDICATOR, not to any
proof -- worth recording, because a checker that cries wolf gets
switched off, which is the failure it exists to prevent:

  * the verdict is not reliably at line start (runners indent, and some
    prefix it with "part 2"), so anchoring there reported "no verdict at
    all" for four sound runners;
  * a FAILED verdict is EXPECTED from the vacuity run, whose point is that
    the witnesses fail, and runners capture both runs in one file -- so
    only non-witness failures count;
  * witnesses are labelled "witness:" as well as "vacuity witness:", and
    matching only the longer form undercounted them;
  * conc_reset reproduces the unsynchronised-RMW observation by design
    under STORE_ORDER=1 and was missing from EXPECT_FAIL.

One real coverage gap was found, and fixed in the harness rather than the
checker: run_all.sh ran rx_recv at the default MC=1, where a
multi-descriptor chain cannot arise, so the witness for one passed
legitimately and the chain path -- where F16's repeat trigger lives -- was
never covered by the sweep.  It now runs at MC=2.
